How RCR is set

RCR starts at 23 (dry). It changes to 12 when any of these is found, and the reason is shown in small type in the box:

  • Rain in the METAR: present weather such as RA, -RA, DZ, SHRA or TSRA.
  • Snow in the METAR: SN or SG.
  • Dewpoint spread of 2°C or less: treated as very low.
  • METAR runway state: damp, wet or snow.
  • CFP weather: rain or snow in the CFP observation.
  • Forecast rain or snow: in the Open-Meteo strip’s “now” or “+1h” cell.
  • D-ATIS: it mentions rain, wet, drizzle, showers, thunderstorms or snow.

Icy conditions (freezing rain, ice, slush, frost, ice pellets) leave the box empty so you enter the value yourself.

An RCR reported in the D-ATIS or METAR is used as reported. A value you type is never overwritten.
